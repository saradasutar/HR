HR Dashboard v1.6.59 — Eight Strength Status choices
====================================================

Available choices:
- Present
- Relieved
- Transferred
- Retired
- Present Oth Off
- Relieved Oth Off
- Transferred Oth Off
- Retired Oth Off

Present and Present Oth Off are treated as active/present statuses. A relieving
date is required for the other six statuses.

Update both parts:
1. Replace Apps Script Code.gs, save it, then edit the existing deployment and
   deploy it as a New version.
2. Replace index.html, styles.css and app.js in the GitHub HR repository.
3. Wait about two minutes, then press Ctrl+Shift+R and sign in again.

Do not run setupHRDashboard and do not replace employee data.
